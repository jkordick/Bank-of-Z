# Substitution audit

## CICS

Scope: CICS, including its DB2/VSAM stores, setup utility, MQ ingress and z/OS Connect seam. IMS and statement-job verdicts are outside this pass.

All 24 rows are `blocked`: INT-Q01 explicitly leaves deployed use, ownership and service contracts open for every integration entry (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:572`). This is not a recommendation to keep everything; no survival or substitution decision is approved while its prerequisites remain unanswered. Each row names additional applicable blockers and the cost of deferral versus premature change.

**Verification boundary:** BNK1CCS, BNK1DCS and CRECUST have control flow **unverified by tooling** (TOOL-Q02, TOOL-Q03, TOOL-Q04); missing generated/IBM members also leave fields unverified (TOOL-Q06). Successful parses do not resolve the recorded XFRFUN/CREACC coverage contradictions (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:552`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:554`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:556`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:557`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:571`).
