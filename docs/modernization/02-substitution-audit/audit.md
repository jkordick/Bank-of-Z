# Substitution audit

## CICS, IMS and batch

Scope: CICS and IMS, including their stores, setup/load utilities, MQ ingress and z/OS Connect seams, followed by batch statements. The single table groups the original 24 CICS rows before the 20 IMS rows and eight batch rows.

All 52 rows are `blocked`: INT-Q01 explicitly leaves deployed use, ownership and service contracts open for every integration entry (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:572`). This is not a recommendation to keep everything; no survival or substitution decision is approved while its prerequisites remain unanswered. Each row names additional applicable blockers and the cost of deferral versus premature change.

**Verification boundary:** BNK1CCS, BNK1DCS and CRECUST have control flow **unverified by tooling** (TOOL-Q02, TOOL-Q03, TOOL-Q04); missing generated/IBM members also leave fields unverified (TOOL-Q06). Successful parses do not resolve the recorded XFRFUN/CREACC coverage contradictions (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:552`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:554`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:556`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:557`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:571`). Non-COBOL/configuration observations below retain direct-source citations from rediscovery; they are not parser-verified facts.

**IMS verification boundary:** IBTRAN has control flow **unverified by tooling**, pending TOOL-Q05 (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:555`). PL/I, Java, JNI semantics, DBD/PSB, JCL and API configuration are outside the COBOL parser's scope; their rows retain the direct-source evidence recorded in rediscovery (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/integrations.md:9`). This pass consumes rediscovery only; it neither resolves open questions nor infers safe retirement from sample/test labels.

**Batch verification boundary:** BNKSTMTC has control flow **unverified by tooling**, pending TOOL-Q01; PL/I and JCL are outside parser scope (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/open-questions.md:551`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/integrations.md:394`). The batch pass consumes B01/B02/B03 and associated data/operational entries only; it does not infer statement rules, production cadence, customer delivery or equivalence of the two job variants (`docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/integrations.md:396`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/integrations.md:404`, `docs/modernization/01-rediscovery/integrations.md:412`).
